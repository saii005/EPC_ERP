# 27. EPC End-to-End Process Map

## Overview
Complete end-to-end process map from project initiation through handover, showing how all processes flow together.

## Process Flow Diagram

```
PROJECT INITIATION
├─ Win Contract
├─ Establish Project
├─ Setup Budget & Schedule
└─ Kickoff Meeting
        ↓
ENGINEERING & DESIGN
├─ Design 30%
├─ Design 60%
├─ Design 90%
├─ Final Design 100%
└─ Client Approval
        ↓
PROCUREMENT
├─ Procurement Planning
├─ RFQ for Long-Lead Items
├─ Bid Evaluation & PO
├─ Material Receipt & QA
└─ Vendor Management
        ↓
CONSTRUCTION
├─ Construction Planning
├─ Daily Work Execution
├─ Quality Inspections
├─ HSE Management
└─ Cost & Schedule Tracking
        ↓
COMMISSIONING
├─ Pre-Commissioning Tests
├─ System Startup
├─ Performance Testing
└─ Personnel Training
        ↓
HANDOVER & CLOSURE
├─ Punch List Completion
├─ Final Inspection
├─ Formal Handover
└─ Project Closeout

```

## Cross-Domain Activities
- **Financial Tracking:** Runs across all phases
- **Document Control:** All phases produce documents
- **Quality Management:** All phases have quality gates
- **HSE Compliance:** Ongoing across construction phase
- **Client Communication:** Throughout project

## Process Integration Points
- Engineering ↔ Procurement: BOM drives procurement
- Procurement ↔ Construction: Material delivery enables construction
- Construction ↔ Quality: Inspections ensure quality
- All phases ↔ Finance: Costs tracked continuously

## Timeline Summary
- Design: 6 months
- Procurement: 15 months (overlapping)
- Construction: 12 months
- Commissioning: 4 months
- Closeout: 2 months
- **Total:** 34+ months
