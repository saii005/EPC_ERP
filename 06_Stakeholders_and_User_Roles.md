# 06. Stakeholders and User Roles

## 📋 Overview

This document defines all stakeholder groups, their roles, responsibilities, and system access requirements in the EPC ERP System.

---

## 1. Executive & Management Stakeholders

### 1.1 Chief Executive Officer (CEO) / Managing Director
**Responsibilities:**
- Approves major contracts and project selection
- Reviews quarterly project portfolio performance
- Escalation point for critical issues
- Sets company strategy and targets

**System Access:**
- Executive dashboard with KPIs
- Project portfolio view
- Financial summary reports
- Limited detail access (high-level metrics only)

**Frequency of Use:** Weekly

---

### 1.2 Chief Finance Officer (CFO)
**Responsibilities:**
- Manages company cash flow and profitability
- Approves project budgets exceeding threshold
- Reviews financial reports and forecasts
- Manages banking relationships and credit facilities

**System Access:**
- Financial dashboard
- Project profitability analysis
- Cash flow forecasts
- Budget vs. actual reports
- Invoice and payment tracking

**Frequency of Use:** Daily

---

### 1.3 Project Director / Vice President (Projects)
**Responsibilities:**
- Oversees multiple concurrent projects
- Ensures project profitability and client satisfaction
- Makes strategic decisions on resource allocation
- Reports to CEO on project status

**System Access:**
- Portfolio dashboard (all projects)
- Project-level status reports
- Cost and schedule performance
- Resource utilization reports
- Escalation approvals (budget, contract changes)

**Frequency of Use:** Daily

---

## 2. Project Management Stakeholders

### 2.1 Project Manager
**Responsibilities:**
- Day-to-day project execution and control
- Budget and schedule management
- Team coordination and task assignment
- Client communication and relationship management
- Risk and issue management
- Ensures project delivers on scope, cost, schedule

**System Access:**
- Project dashboard
- Work breakdown structure and schedules
- Budget tracking (actual vs. plan)
- Team task assignments
- Vendor management interface
- Change order approval/submission
- Team collaboration tools

**Frequency of Use:** Daily

---

### 2.2 Assistant Project Manager / Project Coordinator
**Responsibilities:**
- Administrative support to Project Manager
- Document preparation and distribution
- Meeting scheduling and minutes
- Status report compilation
- Maintain project registers and logs

**System Access:**
- View project data (read-only)
- Create and update administrative records
- Generate project reports
- Document uploads and version control
- Email notifications and alerts

**Frequency of Use:** Daily

---

## 3. Engineering Stakeholders

### 3.1 Engineering Manager / Lead Engineer
**Responsibilities:**
- Leads design and engineering activities
- Manages engineering team and deliverables
- Ensures technical quality and compliance with standards
- Client liaison for technical matters
- Approves engineering specifications and drawings
- Manages design changes and technical queries

**System Access:**
- Engineering deliverable management
- Document control (specifications, drawings)
- Review and approval workflows
- Team assignments and status tracking
- Technical query logs
- Change request submission

**Frequency of Use:** Daily

---

### 3.2 Design Engineer / Discipline Engineer
**Responsibilities:**
- Creates engineering deliverables (specifications, drawings, calculations)
- Conducts design studies and analysis
- Responds to technical queries
- Participates in design reviews
- Updates design basis documents

**System Access:**
- Create and edit specifications, drawings, BOMs
- Submit deliverables for review
- Track review comments and action items
- Access design databases and references
- Upload design calculations and reports

**Frequency of Use:** Daily

---

### 3.3 Engineering Quality Reviewer
**Responsibilities:**
- Reviews engineering deliverables for quality and completeness
- Verifies compliance with standards and best practices
- Approves deliverables before client submission
- Manages design review cycles

**System Access:**
- View deliverables for review
- Add review comments and action items
- Approve/reject deliverables
- Track review cycle metrics
- Archive approved versions

**Frequency of Use:** Weekly

---

## 4. Procurement & Vendor Management Stakeholders

### 4.1 Procurement Manager
**Responsibilities:**
- Develops procurement strategy and vendor selection
- Manages purchasing team
- Approves requisitions and RFQs
- Manages vendor relationships
- Ensures value for money in purchases
- Negotiates contracts and terms

**System Access:**
- Requisition and RFQ management
- Vendor management and scorecards
- Purchase order oversight
- Bid evaluation and award recommendations
- Approval workflows
- Vendor performance reports
- Cost tracking by vendor

**Frequency of Use:** Daily

---

### 4.2 Procurement Specialist / Purchasing Officer
**Responsibilities:**
- Processes purchase requisitions
- Issues RFQs to vendors
- Evaluates vendor quotations
- Creates and issues purchase orders
- Manages PO follow-up and expediting
- Resolves vendor issues and queries

**System Access:**
- Create/edit requisitions and RFQs
- Distribute RFQs to vendors
- Receive and record quotations
- Create purchase orders
- Track PO status
- Log vendor communications
- Generate PO status reports

**Frequency of Use:** Daily

---

### 4.3 Vendor Manager / Vendor Development Officer
**Responsibilities:**
- Pre-qualifies and approves vendors
- Manages vendor scorecards and performance
- Conducts vendor audits and assessments
- Manages vendor compliance and certifications
- Develops strategic vendor partnerships
- Resolves vendor performance issues

**System Access:**
- Vendor master maintenance
- Qualification questionnaires
- Scorecard generation and analysis
- Compliance tracking (certifications, audits)
- Vendor performance reports
- Audit findings tracking
- Vendor portal access

**Frequency of Use:** 3-4 times per week

---

## 5. Material & Logistics Stakeholders

### 5.1 Logistics Manager
**Responsibilities:**
- Manages material flow from suppliers through warehouse to site
- Coordinates shipping and delivery
- Manages warehouse operations and inventory
- Ensures material availability for construction
- Controls material costs and optimizes logistics
- Manages material handling and storage

**System Access:**
- Material receipts and warehouse tracking
- Stock level monitoring
- Transfer and issue-to-site tracking
- Shipping and delivery coordination
- Warehouse layout and bin management
- Shortage and surplus reports
- Material traceability

**Frequency of Use:** Daily

---

### 5.2 Warehouse Manager
**Responsibilities:**
- Manages daily warehouse operations
- Receives and stores materials
- Maintains accurate inventory records
- Issues materials to site
- Conducts physical counts
- Ensures proper storage and safety
- Manages material handling equipment

**System Access:**
- Material receipt and entry
- Material location assignment
- Stock transfers and issues
- Physical count recording
- Material search and lookup
- Material hold/quarantine
- Warehouse reports

**Frequency of Use:** Daily

---

### 5.3 Inventory Controller
**Responsibilities:**
- Maintains inventory accuracy
- Conducts physical counts and reconciliation
- Investigates and resolves inventory variances
- Creates and tracks obsolete material
- Generates inventory reports and analysis
- Supports material traceability requirements

**System Access:**
- Inventory master management
- Physical count planning and entry
- Variance analysis
- Obsolete material management
- Inventory reports and aging
- Material movement audit trail

**Frequency of Use:** 3-4 times per week

---

## 6. Construction & Field Stakeholders

### 6.1 Construction Manager
**Responsibilities:**
- Plans and manages on-site construction execution
- Manages construction team and contractors
- Ensures schedule adherence and quality
- Controls construction costs and budget
- Manages site safety and HSE
- Coordinates with engineering and procurement
- Reports progress to Project Manager

**System Access:**
- Work schedule and activity tracking
- Daily progress reports (DPRs)
- Team and resource assignments
- Material and equipment tracking
- Safety and incident management
- Quality inspection records
- Change order management
- Budget and cost tracking

**Frequency of Use:** Daily

---

### 6.2 Site Supervisor / Foreman
**Responsibilities:**
- Leads daily site operations and work crews
- Assigns daily work and supervises execution
- Records daily progress and productivity
- Manages crew safety and enforces procedures
- Reports issues and changes needed
- Maintains site conditions and housekeeping
- Coordinates with material handlers and inspectors

**System Access:**
- Daily work plan and activity assignments
- Daily progress report entry
- Time and attendance (crew hours)
- Material issues and receipt
- Safety incident/near-miss reporting
- Quality hold points and inspections
- Photo/documentation upload
- Field mobile app for offline data entry

**Frequency of Use:** Daily

---

### 6.3 Safety Officer / HSE Officer
**Responsibilities:**
- Develops and enforces site HSE procedures
- Conducts safety inductions and training
- Inspects site for hazards
- Investigates incidents and near-misses
- Maintains HSE records and compliance
- Conducts safety audits
- Reports HSE metrics to management

**System Access:**
- HSE incident and near-miss reporting
- Induction and training records
- Safety audit checklists and findings
- PPE and equipment tracking
- Safety alerts and notifications
- HSE metrics and trending
- Permit-to-Work system

**Frequency of Use:** Daily

---

## 7. Quality & Compliance Stakeholders

### 7.1 Quality Manager / Quality Assurance Manager
**Responsibilities:**
- Establishes quality standards and procedures
- Develops inspection and test plans
- Manages quality team
- Approves quality certifications
- Manages quality improvement initiatives
- Conducts quality audits
- Reports quality metrics to management

**System Access:**
- Inspection and Test Plan (ITP) creation
- Quality inspection records
- Non-Conformance Report (NCR) management
- Quality certification
- Quality trending and reporting
- Quality metrics dashboard
- Audit management

**Frequency of Use:** Daily

---

### 7.2 Quality Inspector / QC Inspector
**Responsibilities:**
- Conducts inspections per ITPs
- Records inspection results
- Collects inspection evidence
- Reports defects and non-conformances
- Participates in corrective action follow-up
- Maintains inspection equipment and calibration
- Provides quality feedback to teams

**System Access:**
- Inspection checklist and report entry
- Photo/document attachment
- Test result recording
- NCR initiation
- Inspection schedule tracking
- Equipment calibration records
- Mobile field app for inspections

**Frequency of Use:** Daily

---

## 8. Finance & Administration Stakeholders

### 8.1 Finance Manager / Controller
**Responsibilities:**
- Manages project financial performance
- Prepares monthly cost/financial reports
- Manages project budgets and forecasts
- Generates financial statements
- Ensures compliance with accounting standards
- Manages cash flow and working capital

**System Access:**
- Cost tracking and reporting
- Budget management and analysis
- Forecast and variance reporting
- GL integration and account management
- Financial statement generation
- Payment approval workflows
- Audit reports for compliance

**Frequency of Use:** Daily

---

### 8.2 Finance Analyst / Accountant
**Responsibilities:**
- Records project costs and transactions
- Processes invoices and payments
- Maintains cost records
- Generates cost reports
- Performs cost analysis
- Supports financial planning and forecasting

**System Access:**
- Cost entry and transaction recording
- Invoice and payment processing
- Cost report generation
- Budget vs. actual analysis
- GL account management
- Cost reconciliation

**Frequency of Use:** Daily

---

### 8.3 HR / Resource Manager
**Responsibilities:**
- Manages human resources and recruitment
- Allocates personnel to projects
- Tracks employee utilization and availability
- Manages payroll and benefits
- Supports training and development
- Manages employee records

**System Access:**
- Employee master data
- Resource allocation and assignment
- Utilization and availability tracking
- Leave and absence management
- Training records
- Payroll integration
- HR dashboards and reports

**Frequency of Use:** 3-4 times per week

---

## 9. Client & External Stakeholders

### 9.1 Client Project Manager / Owner's Representative
**Responsibilities:**
- Represents client interests on project
- Approves engineering deliverables
- Reviews project progress and status
- Approves change orders and variations
- Authorizes payments and billing
- Communicates client requirements and decisions

**System Access:**
- Read-only access to project information
- Deliverable submission and review portal
- Project status visibility
- Progress reports and dashboards
- Change order review and approval
- Invoice review (for billing disputes)
- Secure document sharing

**Frequency of Use:** 2-3 times per week

---

### 9.2 Subcontractor / Vendor
**Responsibilities:**
- Executes work or supplies goods per contract
- Submits required documentation and certs
- Reports progress and issues
- Participates in inspections
- Manages subcontractor team

**System Access:**
- Vendor portal (read-only)
- PO and contract terms
- Invoice submission
- Status tracking
- Inspection and compliance documentation
- Secure document uploads
- Communication and notifications

**Frequency of Use:** 1-2 times per week

---

## 10. System Administration Stakeholders

### 10.1 System Administrator
**Responsibilities:**
- Manages system users and access
- Configures system settings
- Maintains data integrity
- Manages backups and recovery
- Monitors system performance
- Supports end users

**System Access:**
- User management (create, modify, delete)
- Role and permission configuration
- System configuration and setup
- Database administration
- Backup and restore
- System monitoring and alerts
- Audit logs and access tracking

**Frequency of Use:** 5 times per week

---

### 10.2 System Analyst / Business Analyst
**Responsibilities:**
- Supports system implementation
- Gathers business requirements
- Configures system to meet requirements
- Trains users
- Provides business user support
- Documents system processes

**System Access:**
- Full access for testing and configuration
- User training environment
- System documentation and logs
- Workflow and form configuration
- Custom report builder
- Data migration and import tools

**Frequency of Use:** Daily during implementation

---

## 11. Stakeholder Communication Plan

| Stakeholder | Frequency | Method | Content |
|-------------|-----------|--------|---------|
| Executive | Monthly | Dashboard/Report | KPIs, profitability, risks |
| Project Manager | Daily | System/Meeting | Status, actuals, issues |
| Engineering | Daily | System/Meetings | Deliverable status, approvals |
| Procurement | Daily | System | Requisitions, RFQs, orders |
| Finance | Weekly | Report | Costs, budget, forecasts |
| Quality | Daily | System | Inspections, NCRs, compliance |
| HSE | Daily | System | Incidents, audits, compliance |
| Client | Bi-weekly | Report/Portal | Progress, status, deliverables |
