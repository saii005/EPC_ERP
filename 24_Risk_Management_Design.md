# 24. Risk Management Design

## Overview
Risk Management covers project risk identification, analysis, mitigation, and monitoring to proactively manage project uncertainties.

## Risk Categories
- **Technical Risks:** Equipment selection, technology, feasibility
- **Schedule Risks:** Long lead times, critical path delays
- **Cost Risks:** Price escalation, cost overruns, waste
- **Quality Risks:** Design flaws, workmanship, testing gaps
- **HSE Risks:** Safety incidents, environmental issues
- **Commercial Risks:** Contract disputes, payment delays
- **Political/Regulatory Risks:** Regulatory changes, political instability
- **Resource Risks:** Key person dependencies, skill gaps

## Risk Process
1. **Identification:** Identify potential risks
2. **Analysis:** Assess probability and impact
3. **Prioritization:** Rank risks by significance
4. **Mitigation Planning:** Develop response strategies
5. **Implementation:** Execute mitigation actions
6. **Monitoring:** Track risk status regularly
7. **Updates:** Revise risk list as situation changes

## Risk Assessment
- **Probability:** Low (0-33%), Medium (33-66%), High (66-100%)
- **Impact:** Low ($1-50K), Medium ($50-250K), High (>$250K)
- **Risk Score:** Probability × Impact
- **Priority:** High-risk items get immediate attention

## Risk Responses
- **Avoid:** Eliminate the risk through different approach
- **Mitigate:** Reduce probability or impact
- **Transfer:** Insurance or outsource to reduce risk
- **Accept:** Accept the risk and consequences

## Risk Monitoring
- **Risk Register:** Tracked risks and mitigation status
- **Trigger Events:** Indicators that risk is materializing
- **Review Frequency:** Monthly risk reviews
- **Escalation:** Notify management if risks escalate
- **Closure:** Close risks when threat passes
