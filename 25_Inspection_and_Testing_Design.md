# 25. Inspection and Testing Design

## Overview
Inspection & Testing covers development of Inspection & Test Plans (ITPs), execution of inspections and tests, and recording of results.

## ITP Development
- **Based on Specifications:** Review spec to identify critical characteristics
- **Sampling Plan:** Determine percentage of items to inspect
- **Hold Points:** Define where work must stop for inspection
- **Acceptance Criteria:** Clear pass/fail criteria
- **Documentation:** Inspection reports and sign-offs required
- **Timeline:** Schedule inspections with project timeline

## Inspection Types
- **Receiving Inspection:** Verify vendor-supplied materials
- **In-Process Inspection:** Inspect during work execution
- **Final Inspection:** Verify completed work meets spec
- **Witness Inspection:** Client or third-party witnesses inspection
- **Audit Inspection:** Verify compliance with procedures

## Test Types
- **Functional Test:** Verify equipment operates
- **Performance Test:** Verify equipment meets performance spec
- **Non-Destructive Testing (NDT):** UT, RT, MPI, dye penetrant
- **Pressure Testing:** Test pressure vessels and piping
- **Environmental Testing:** Test under operating conditions

## Inspection Documentation
- **Inspection Checklists:** Step-by-step checklist of inspection items
- **Inspection Reports:** Record of inspection results
- **Certificates of Conformance:** Material compliance certificates
- **Test Reports:** Test data and results
- **Photographs:** Visual evidence of inspections and tests

## Non-Conformance
- **Defect:** Item does not meet specification
- **NCR Creation:** Document non-conformance
- **Investigation:** Root cause analysis
- **Corrective Action:** Plan to fix non-conformance
- **Re-inspection:** Verify correction effective
- **Closure:** Close NCR once verified

## Quality Certifications
- Certs of Conformance for materials
- Welder qualification certs
- Equipment performance test reports
- Final quality acceptance sign-off
