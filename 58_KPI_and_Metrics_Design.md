# 58. KPI and Metrics Design

## Overview
Key performance indicators (KPIs) and metrics framework for executive/project monitoring.

## KPI Categories
- **Project Performance:** CPI, SPI, Earned Value
- **Procurement:** On-time delivery, cost variance, lead time
- **Construction:** Productivity, safety metrics
- **Quality:** Defect rate, inspection pass rate
- **Financial:** Cash flow, profitability, invoice aging
- **HSE:** Near-miss frequency, recordable incidents
