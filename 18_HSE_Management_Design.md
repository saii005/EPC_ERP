# 18. HSE Management Design

## Overview
HSE (Health, Safety & Environment) Management covers safety procedures, incident management, environmental compliance, and personnel safety to maintain zero-harm culture.

## Key Functions
- **HSE Planning:** Develop site HSE plan, hazard assessment
- **Induction & Training:** Personnel safety training and certification
- **Incident Reporting:** Report and investigate incidents, near-misses
- **Corrective Actions:** Develop and implement corrective actions
- **Inspections & Audits:** Regular HSE audits and inspections
- **Metrics & Reporting:** HSE KPIs and compliance reporting
- **Compliance:** Meet OSHA, local, and client HSE requirements

## HSE Topics
- **Work Safety:** Fall protection, scaffolding, confined spaces
- **Hazardous Operations:** Hot work, heavy lifting, excavation
- **Environmental:** Spill prevention, waste management, air quality
- **Occupational Health:** Medical surveillance, hearing protection
- **Incident Management:** Injury management, root cause analysis
- **HSE Culture:** Promote safety awareness, near-miss reporting

## Safety Meetings
- **Daily Toolbox Talks:** 5-10 minute safety briefings
- **Weekly Safety Meetings:** Formal safety reviews
- **Monthly HSE Reviews:** Executive safety reviews
- **Safety Committees:** Worker participation in safety decisions
