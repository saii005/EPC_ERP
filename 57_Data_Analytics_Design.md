# 57. Data Analytics Design

## Overview
Design for EPC data analytics and business intelligence (BI) integration.

## Analytics Scope
- **Project KPIs:** Cost/schedule variance, progress tracking
- **Procurement Analytics:** Vendor performance, price trends, savings
- **Quality Analytics:** NCR trends, defect types, inspection performance
- **HSE Analytics:** Incident rates, hazard trends
- **Financial Analytics:** Margin analysis, cash flow forecasting

## Data Pipeline
1. **Source:** ERP transactional data
2. **Transform:** ETL processes to cleanse and aggregate
3. **Load:** Warehouse for analytics
4. **Visualize:** Dashboards and custom reports
