# 76. Development Sprint Plan

Development is organized into 2-week agile sprints:
* **Sprints 1 - 2:** Core Project WBS, CBS, and Budgeting structures.
* **Sprints 3 - 4:** Engineering Deliverable Register, IFR/IFA/IFC workflows, and document control.
* **Sprints 5 - 6:** Procurement pipeline (MR, RFQ, TBE, PO, Subcontracts).
* **Sprints 7 - 8:** Material management, site warehousing, and heat-number traceability.
* **Sprints 9 - 10:** Construction Daily Progress Reports (DPR), QA/QC Inspection Test Plans (ITP), and HSE Incident logs.
* **Sprints 11 - 12:** Executive dashboards, Earned Value Management (EVM) analytics, and reporting.
