# 79. User Training & Documentation

## 1. Training Strategy
* **Role-Based Training Programs:** Tailored curriculums for Project Managers, Procurement Officers, Site Engineers, Document Controllers, and Finance Teams.
* **Train-the-Trainer Model:** Empowering internal super-users within the organization to provide continuous peer support.
* **Artifacts:** Video walkthroughs, quick reference guides (QRGs), step-by-step Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) hosted on the internal wiki.
