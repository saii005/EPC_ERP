# 21. Contract Management Design

## Overview
Contract Management covers vendor contracts, subcontracts, and client contracts to manage commercial relationships and obligations.

## Contract Types
- **Vendor Supply Agreements:** Equipment and material supply terms
- **Subcontracts:** Work performance by external contractors
- **Framework Agreements:** Long-term arrangements with terms
- **Service Contracts:** Engineering, testing, training services
- **Maintenance Contracts:** Post-warranty support and maintenance

## Contract Components
- **Scope of Work:** Clear definition of deliverables
- **Pricing & Payment Terms:** Cost and payment schedule
- **Timeline:** Delivery dates and milestones
- **Quality Requirements:** Performance standards and acceptance criteria
- **Warranty & Liability:** Guarantees and limits of liability
- **Termination Conditions:** How contract can be ended
- **Change Management:** How scope changes are handled
- **Dispute Resolution:** Process for resolving disagreements

## Contract Lifecycle
1. **Drafting:** Develop contract terms
2. **Negotiation:** Agree on terms with vendor/contractor
3. **Approval:** Internal approvals and authorization
4. **Execution:** Signatures and authorization
5. **Implementation:** Manage performance and payment
6. **Closure:** Final settlement and close-out
7. **Archive:** Store contract records

## Change Orders
- **Scope Changes:** Modifications to deliverables
- **Schedule Changes:** Extension or acceleration
- **Price Changes:** Cost adjustments
- **Process:** Document, analyze impact, approve, execute, archive

## Performance Monitoring
- **Milestone Tracking:** Verify completion of contract milestones
- **Quality Verification:** Ensure deliverables meet contract spec
- **Payment Approval:** Verify work before payment
- **Dispute Resolution:** Handle claims and disputes
