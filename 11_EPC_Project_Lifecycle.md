# 11. EPC Project Lifecycle

## 📋 Overview

This document describes the complete lifecycle of an EPC project from initial concept through handover and closeout. It shows how all domains (Engineering, Procurement, Construction) flow together.

---

## Phase 1: Bid & Contract (Months -6 to 0)

**Objective:** Win contract and establish project baseline

### Key Activities
- Prepare bid and cost estimate
- Negotiate contract terms
- Secure client signatures and financing
- Establish project governance
- Allocate project team
- Create project master schedule and budget
- Conduct project kickoff

### Deliverables
- Signed contract
- Project charter
- Work breakdown structure
- Master schedule
- Approved budget
- Resource plan
- Project team directory

**Duration:** 3-6 months  
**Team Size:** 5-10 people

---

## Phase 2: Front-End Engineering & Design (FEED) (Months 0-6)

**Objective:** Complete detailed engineering design to 100%

### Activities by Domain

#### Engineering
- Finalize design basis and design calculations
- Complete P&IDs, GA drawings, equipment schedules
- Develop specifications (process, mechanical, electrical, I&C)
- Perform design reviews (30%, 60%, 90%)
- Obtain client approvals (IFA, IFC)
- Create Bill of Materials
- Address technical queries from client/procurement

#### Procurement Planning
- Analyze BOM to identify long-lead items
- Develop procurement strategy
- Pre-qualify critical vendors
- Issue early RFQs for long-lead items
- Begin vendor selection for strategic items

#### Construction Planning
- Conduct constructability reviews
- Identify construction logistics and resource needs
- Preliminary construction schedule
- Site survey and facility assessment

### Key Milestones
- Month 2: 30% Design Review
- Month 4: 60% Design Review
- Month 5: 90% Design Review
- Month 6: 100% Design Complete & Client Approval

**Team Size:** 15-25 people  
**Budget Consumption:** 10-15% of project cost

---

## Phase 3: Procurement (Months 3-18)

**Objective:** Source all materials, equipment, and subcontracts

### Activities

**Early Procurement (Months 3-6)**
- RFQ for long-lead equipment (>24 weeks)
- Bid evaluation and PO issuance
- Expediting of critical deliveries
- Factory inspections for major equipment

**Main Procurement (Months 6-15)**
- RFQ for bulk materials
- Vendor selection and bid evaluation
- PO issuance for all items
- Quality assurance during manufacturing
- Arrange shipping and logistics

**Late Procurement (Months 15-18)**
- Final materials and consumables
- On-site support contractor RFQs
- Warehouse setup

### Key Deliverables
- Purchase orders for all items
- Vendor agreements
- Material certs of conformance
- Inspection/test reports
- Shipping documentation

### Procurement Timeline Coordination
- Long-lead items: RFQ at 20% design, PO by month 4
- Standard items: RFQ at 60% design, PO by month 8
- Site consumables: RFQ at 80% design, PO by month 14

**Budget Consumption:** 40-50% of project cost

---

## Phase 4: Construction Preparation (Months 12-18)

**Objective:** Plan and prepare for on-site execution

### Activities
- Detailed construction methodology development
- Erection procedure preparation
- Equipment installation sequence planning
- Craft labor requisition and recruitment
- Safety and HSE plan development
- Site mobilization planning
- Inspection and test plan development
- Training and induction material preparation

### Deliverables
- Construction execution plan
- Detailed work breakdown structure
- Resource loading schedule
- Equipment lay-down plan
- Safety procedures
- Site organization plan

**Team Size:** 10-15 people  
**Budget Consumption:** 2-3% of project cost

---

## Phase 5: Construction & Installation (Months 18-30)

**Objective:** Execute physical work on-site, achieving schedule and quality

### Activities

**Work Streams Execute in Parallel**
1. **Structural Work:** Foundations, building structures, piping racks
2. **Mechanical Installation:** Equipment mounting, piping, support structures
3. **Electrical Installation:** Cable runs, terminations, panel assembly
4. **I&C Installation:** Instrumentation, control panels, wiring
5. **Testing Preparation:** Equipment functional tests, system checks

### Daily Management
- Daily progress reports (DPRs)
- Daily toolbox talks and safety briefings
- Timekeeping and productivity tracking
- Material issue-to-site and consumption
- Inspection and test execution
- Quality non-conformance management
- Incident and near-miss reporting
- Schedule and budget tracking

### Key Milestones
- Month 20: Structural work 50% complete
- Month 22: Mechanical 30% complete
- Month 24: Electrical work started
- Month 26: All mechanical work complete
- Month 28: All electrical work complete
- Month 30: All installation complete

**Site Team Size:** 30-100+ people  
**Budget Consumption:** 35-45% of project cost

---

## Phase 6: Pre-Commissioning, Commissioning & Testing (Months 28-32)

**Objective:** Verify all systems function per specification and client requirements

### Pre-Commissioning (Months 28-30)
- Equipment pre-commissioning and function testing
- System checks and calibrations
- Spare parts verification
- Documentation package assembly
- Operations manual preparation
- Personnel training preparation

### Commissioning (Months 30-32)
- System startup and initial operation
- Performance testing under operating conditions
- Troubleshooting and resolution of issues
- Fine-tuning and optimization
- Performance acceptance testing with client

### Testing Activities
- Mechanical completion testing
- Operational performance testing
- Safety system testing
- Process parameter verification
- Environmental monitoring

### Key Deliverables
- Commissioning plan and procedures
- Test reports and data sheets
- Punch list of outstanding items
- Training records
- Operations and maintenance manuals
- Final acceptance certificate

**Team Size:** 15-25 people

---

## Phase 7: Handover & Project Closure (Months 32-34)

**Objective:** Transition project to client and formally close project

### Activities

**Pre-Handover**
- Complete all punch list items
- Final training session with client operations team
- Documentation hand-over package preparation
- Equipment manuals and spare parts handover
- Warranty terms clarification
- Post-warranty support agreement

**Handover**
- Formal sign-off from client
- Transfer of keys and access
- Operations transition
- Transition of on-site support team (if required)

**Project Closure**
- Financial reconciliation (final cost analysis)
- Vendor contract closure and final payments
- Personnel release and demobilization
- Project documentation archival
- Lessons learned documentation
- Post-project review meeting

### Deliverables
- Handover certificate (signed by client)
- Final cost report
- Final schedule report
- Lessons learned report
- Operator training certificates
- Equipment warranties and manuals
- Final payment records

**Duration:** 2-4 months

---

## Schedule Dependency Critical Path

```
Start → Design (0-6M) → Procurement (3-18M) → Construction (18-30M) 
        → Commissioning (28-32M) → Handover (32-34M) → End
```

**Critical Path:** Design → Long-lead procurement RFQ → PO → Delivery → Construction → Testing

**Longest Lead Item Examples:**
- Custom equipment: 24-30 weeks
- Specialty materials: 16-20 weeks
- Bulk materials: 8-12 weeks
