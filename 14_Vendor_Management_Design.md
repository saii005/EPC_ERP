# 14. Vendor Management Design

## 📋 Overview

This document details the Vendor Management system, covering vendor qualification, performance monitoring, compliance tracking, and vendor development strategies.

---

## 1. Vendor Management Process

```
Identify Vendor Need
        ↓
Pre-qualification Assessment
        ↓
Vendor Questionnaire
        ↓
Evaluate Responses
        ↓
Site Audit (if required)
        ↓
Add to Approved Vendor List
        ↓
Monitor Performance
        ↓
Annual Re-assessment
```

---

## 2. Vendor Qualification Criteria

### 2.1 Financial Criteria
- **Financial Stability:** Recent financial statements, credit rating >BBB
- **Insurance:** Product liability, workers' comp, property insurance
- **Banking References:** Bank credit line, payment history
- **Assets:** Sufficient assets for size of business

### 2.2 Quality Criteria
- **ISO 9001 Certification** or equivalent quality management system
- **Quality Records:** <2% defect rate, traceability capability
- **Testing Capability:** Ability to test products to spec
- **Audits:** Willingness to be audited

### 2.3 Technical Criteria
- **Equipment/Materials:** List of what they can supply
- **Capacity:** Can they meet our volume requirements?
- **Lead Times:** Standard lead times for their products
- **Technical Support:** Engineering support available?

### 2.4 HSE Criteria
- **Safety Record:** <5 lost-time incidents/year
- **HSE Certifications:** OHSAS 18001 or equivalent
- **Site Safety:** Compliance with site safety requirements
- **Environmental:** Compliance with environmental regulations

### 2.5 Compliance Criteria
- **Regulatory:** Compliance with applicable regulations
- **Import/Export:** Ability to handle customs/tariffs
- **Documentation:** Ability to provide required certs and docs
- **Traceability:** Ability to trace materials/components

## 3. Vendor Qualification Process

### 3.1 Initial Vendor Questionnaire
Sent to prospective vendors covering:
- Company history and experience
- Financial information
- Quality certifications
- HSE record and certifications
- References from similar projects
- Capacity and lead times
- Pricing for standard items

### 3.2 Evaluation Scoring
```
Financial Evaluation:        /25 points
Quality Management:          /25 points
Technical Capability:        /20 points
HSE/Compliance:             /20 points
References/Reputation:      /10 points
                            --------
                Total:       /100 points

Passing Score: >75 points
```

### 3.3 Site Audit
For vendors scoring 75-85 or supplying critical items:
- On-site facility visit
- Quality system audit
- HSE compliance check
- Capacity assessment
- References verification

## 4. Vendor Performance Monitoring

### 4.1 Scorecard Metrics
- **On-Time Delivery:** % of POs delivered by promised date
- **Quality:** % of deliveries with zero defects
- **Documentation:** % of invoices with complete documentation
- **Responsiveness:** Average response time to queries
- **Cost:** Price competitiveness vs. market average
- **Communication:** Compliance with communication standards
- **Compliance:** Adherence to contract terms

### 4.2 Scorecard Calculation
```
Monthly Scorecard:
┌──────────────────────────────────┐
│ On-Time Delivery:    80% = 80 pts │
│ Quality:             95% = 95 pts │
│ Documentation:       90% = 90 pts │
│ Responsiveness:      3 days = 85 pts
│ Cost:                98% of avg = 98 pts
│                                    ────
│ Total Score:         448 / 5 = 89.6 pts
│                                    ────
│ Performance: GOOD (85-95 range)   │
└──────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 4.3 Performance Categories
- **Excellent:** >95 points - Preferred vendor, can award without competitive bid
- **Good:** 85-95 points - Continue using, no corrective action needed
- **Acceptable:** 75-85 points - Monitor closely, required improvement plan
- **Poor:** <75 points - Formal corrective action, possible suspension
- **Critical:** 2 consecutive poor scorecards - Suspension or removal

## 5. Vendor Compliance Management

### 5.1 Certifications Tracking
- ISO 9001 (Quality)
- ISO 14001 (Environmental)
- OHSAS 18001 / ISO 45001 (Safety)
- ASME (for pressure equipment)
- API (for oil/gas products)
- Industry-specific certifications
- Expiration tracking and renewal reminders

### 5.2 Audit Schedule
- Annual audit: All active vendors
- Bi-annual: High-volume vendors, critical suppliers
- Upon Request: Compliance audits, special audits
- Unannounced: Selected vendors as part of audit strategy

### 5.3 Corrective Actions
When vendor performance drops:
1. Issue warning/notice of concern
2. Request corrective action plan
3. Set timeline for improvement (typically 30-60 days)
4. Monitor implementation
5. Verify effectiveness
6. Close corrective action if successful
7. Escalate if not improved

## 7. Vendor Portal

### 7.1 Portal Features
- **PO Management:** View all POs, status, terms, contact
- **Shipment Tracking:** Track deliveries, provide ETAs
- **Invoice Management:** Submit invoices, track payment status
- **Documents:** Upload certificates, test reports, datasheets
- **Performance:** View scorecard and feedback
- **Messaging:** Communicate with company personnel
- **Portal Analytics:** View trends in orders and performance

### 7.2 Portal Access
- Username/password or SSO integration
- Role-based access (vendor administrator, operators)
- Document encryption and secure transmission
- Audit trail of all portal activity

## 10. Vendor Dispute & Resolution

### 10.1 Dispute Types
- Quality disputes (defects, non-conformances)
- Delivery disputes (late delivery, short shipment)
- Price disputes (invoicing errors, unapproved charges)
- Contract disputes (payment terms, warranty claims)

### 10.2 Resolution Process
1. Document the dispute with evidence
2. Contact vendor to understand their position
3. If not resolved, escalate to Vendor Manager
4. Review contract terms and history
5. Propose resolution or arbitration
6. Document resolution and lessons learned
7. Implement preventive measures
