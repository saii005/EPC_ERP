# 60. AI and ML Opportunity Design

## Overview
Design for AI/ML integration into EPC project lifecycle.

## AI Opportunities
- **Cost Forecasting:** ML-based predictions of project completion costs
- **Schedule Optimization:** ML recommendations for schedule acceleration
- **Quality Analysis:** Image recognition to identify defects
- **Vendor Risk:** Predictive modeling for vendor failure risk
- **Document Analysis:** Automated review of contracts/specs
