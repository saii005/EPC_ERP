# 17. Quality Management Design

## Overview
Quality Management covers inspection planning, test execution, non-conformance management, and quality certifications to ensure all deliverables meet specifications and client requirements.

## Key Functions
- **ITP Development:** Inspection and Test Plans based on specifications
- **Inspection Execution:** Conduct inspections at defined hold points
- **Non-Conformance:** Create, investigate, and close NCRs
- **Quality Metrics:** Trending and reporting of quality performance
- **Certifications:** Material certs of conformance, test reports
- **Quality Audits:** Process audits and quality system audits

## Inspection Levels
- **Hold Point Inspections:** Work pauses for inspection before proceeding
- **Witness Inspections:** Client or Engineer witnesses inspection
- **Non-Destructive Testing (NDT):** UT, RT, magnetic particle, dye penetrant
- **Functional Testing:** Equipment operates per specification
- **Performance Testing:** System achieves specified performance

## Quality Certification
- Material Certificates of Conformance (CoC)
- Test Reports (pressure tests, material analysis)
- Equipment Acceptance Certificates
- Welding Certificates (welder qualification)
- Dimensional Verification Reports
- Final Quality Acceptance
