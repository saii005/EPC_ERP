# 26. Commissioning and Handover Design

## Overview
Commissioning & Handover covers transition from construction to operations, including startup testing, training, and formal handover to client.

## Pre-Commissioning (Construction → Commissioning)
- **Equipment Pre-Testing:** Verify each equipment operates independently
- **System Pre-Testing:** Test systems without full load/operating conditions
- **Documentation Package:** Assemble all manuals, drawings, test reports
- **Personnel Training:** Train operations and maintenance personnel
- **Spare Parts:** Verify spare parts package complete
- **Operating Procedures:** Finalize O&M manuals

## Commissioning Activities
- **System Startup:** Initial system startup under operating conditions
- **Performance Testing:** Verify system meets performance spec
- **Load Testing:** Gradually increase to full operating load
- **Troubleshooting:** Address any issues during startup
- **Fine-Tuning:** Optimize system performance
- **Documentation:** Record test results and lessons learned

## Testing During Commissioning
- **Performance Acceptance Test (PAT):** Verify system meets contract performance
- **Process Parameter Verification:** Verify operating parameters
- **Safety System Testing:** Test safety interlocks and shutdowns
- **Environmental Monitoring:** Monitor environmental compliance
- **Efficiency Testing:** Verify energy efficiency targets

## Training
- **Operations Training:** Teach operations team how to operate system
- **Maintenance Training:** Train maintenance team for troubleshooting
- **Safety Training:** Safety procedures and emergency response
- **Training Records:** Certification of trained personnel
- **Training Materials:** Manuals, videos, procedures

## Punch List Management
- **Identification:** Identify outstanding work or defects
- **Tracking:** Track punch list items to closure
- **Inspection:** Inspect items when corrected
- **Re-test:** Re-test if functional impact
- **Closure:** Sign-off punch list complete

## Formal Handover
- **Handover Meeting:** Meeting to transfer responsibility
- **Documentation Transfer:** All project records to client
- **Equipment Transfer:** Physical transfer of keys, access codes
- **Warranty Transfer:** Handover of warranty documentation
- **Formal Sign-Off:** Client sign-off of completion and acceptance
- **Handover Certificate:** Formal document of handover

## Post-Handover Support
- **Warranty Period:** Support during 1-year warranty period
- **Bug Fixes:** Correct defects discovered post-handover
- **Optimization:** Fine-tune for optimal performance
- **Training:** Refresher training if needed

## Handover Checklist
- All construction work complete and inspected
- All tests performed and documented
- All manuals and drawings provided
- All spare parts provided
- All training completed and documented
- All punch list items complete
- Insurance and warranties in place
- Keys and access codes transferred
- Final financial settlement complete
