# 16. Construction Domain Design

## 📋 Overview

This document describes the Construction domain, covering execution planning, daily progress tracking, resource management, and on-site operational management.

---

## 1. Construction Domain Scope

- **Work Planning:** Daily work plans, crew assignments
- **Progress Tracking:** Daily progress reports, schedule tracking
- **Resource Management:** Labor, equipment, material allocation
- **Quality Control:** Inspection and test execution
- **HSE Management:** Safety, incidents, compliance
- **Change Management:** Change orders, scope management
- **Cost Control:** Labor costs, material consumption, budget tracking

---

## 2. Construction Planning

### 2.1 Construction Strategy
- **Sequencing:** Logical order of work activities
- **Crew Sizing:** Number and skills of workers
- **Equipment Requirements:** Cranes, welding machines, etc.
- **Critical Path:** Activities that determine project finish date
- **Logistics Planning:** Material delivery sequence, site access
- **Safety Planning:** Hazard identification, control measures
- **Quality Planning:** Inspection and test sequencing

### 2.2 Work Breakdown
Master schedule broken into:
- **Phase:** Major work stages (foundation, mechanical, electrical)
- **Activity:** Specific work (install pipe rack, connect piping)
- **Task:** Daily work assigned to crew (Day: Install 50m of pipe)

---

## 3. Daily Progress Management

### 3.1 Daily Progress Report (DPR)
Each day, Site Supervisor records:
- **Date & Location:** Work site and area
- **Activities Completed:** Description of work done
- **Percentage Complete:** % of activity finished today
- **Labor:** Crew size and hours worked
- **Materials:** Materials consumed or received
- **Equipment Used:** Equipment deployed and hours
- **Safety:** No incidents, near-misses, or safety breaches
- **Quality:** Inspections passed/failed, any issues
- **Issues/Delays:** Problems encountered, impacts
- **Photos:** Photographic evidence of progress

### 3.2 Schedule Performance Tracking
- Planned hours vs. actual hours
- Planned activities vs. completed activities
- Schedule variance analysis
- Trending of schedule performance
- Forecasting project completion date

### 3.3 Labor Productivity
- Hours worked per activity
- Work hours per unit produced (e.g., hours per meter of pipe)
- Crew efficiency vs. baseline
- Identifying productivity issues
- Corrective actions for delays

---

## 4. Resource Management

### 4.1 Labor Management
- **Skilled Trades:** Welders, pipefitters, electricians, etc.
- **Supervisory:** Foreman, Site Supervisor, Safety Officer
- **Unskilled Labor:** General laborers, helpers
- **Specialist:** Crane operators, test engineers

### 4.2 Resource Allocation
- Assign craft workers to activities
- Match skills to job requirements
- Optimize crew sizes for efficiency
- Manage overtime for schedule recovery
- Track utilization and productivity

### 4.3 Equipment Management
- **Cranes:** Track deployment, availability, rental costs
- **Welding Equipment:** Maintenance, calibration, availability
- **Vehicles:** Pickup trucks, forklifts for logistics
- **Temporary Facilities:** Office, toilets, break areas, storage
- **Safety Equipment:** Scaffolding, fall protection, etc.

---

## 5. Quality & Inspection

### 5.1 Inspection & Test Plan (ITP)
- Define inspection hold points
- Specify inspection checklist
- Assign inspector responsibilities
- Timeline for inspections
- Documentation requirements
- Re-inspection after corrections

### 5.2 Daily Quality Activities
- Visual inspections during work
- Dimensional checks per spec
- Material verification (correct grade/spec)
- Welding inspections (visual, UT, RT as required)
- Functional testing of equipment
- Documentation of inspection results

### 5.3 Non-Conformance Management
- Identify defects/non-conformances
- Create NCR documenting issue
- Analyze root cause
- Develop corrective action
- Re-inspect to verify correction
- Close NCR
- Track trends for quality improvement

---

## 6. HSE Management

### 6.1 Site Safety
- **Pre-Mobilization:** Site safety briefing, hazard identification
- **Ongoing:** Daily toolbox talks, hazard monitoring, incident response
- **Inspections:** Regular safety audits and audits
- **Training:** Refresher training, new equipment training

### 6.2 Incident Management
- **Reporting:** Any incident reported immediately to HSE Officer
- **Investigation:** Root cause analysis
- **Corrective Actions:** Preventive measures to avoid recurrence
- **Documentation:** Incident record for compliance
- **Closure:** Verify corrective actions effective

### 6.3 HSE Metrics
- Total hours worked
- Lost-time injuries
- Medical treatment injuries
- Near-misses
- Unsafe acts observed and corrected
- Safety audit findings

---

## 7. Change Order Management

### 7.1 Change Request Initiation
- **Identified by:** Site team, Engineer, Client, or PM
- **Description:** Clear description of change needed
- **Justification:** Why change is needed
- **Impacts:** Cost, schedule, scope impacts

### 7.2 Change Order Process
1. Document change request
2. Analyze impacts (scope, cost, schedule, quality, HSE)
3. Estimate additional cost and schedule impact
4. Route for approvals
5. Get client approval if contractual
6. Implement approved changes
7. Update schedule and budget
8. Archive change documentation

### 7.3 Change Categories
- **No Cost/Schedule:** Clarifications, minor revisions
- **Cost Impact:** Additional cost, no schedule impact
- **Schedule Impact:** Extension of completion date
- **Scope Change:** Addition or deletion of work
- **Client Change:** Changes originating from client (usually chargeable)

---

## 8. Construction Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Schedule Performance | On time ±5% | Monthly |
| Quality (Pass First Time) | >95% | Daily |
| Labor Productivity | Per baseline | Weekly |
| Safety (Lost-Time Incidents) | Zero | Ongoing |
| Material Shortage | <2% | Monthly |
| Cost Variance | ±5% | Monthly |
